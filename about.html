Hello! My name is Yuliia.

Welcome to my travel blog! I’m here to share my experiences of exploring new places, meeting fascinating people, and discovering the beauty and culture this world has to offer.

Through storytelling, photos, and personal insights, I hope to inspire you to embrace the adventure of travel. Whether you're looking for tips, motivation, or just a good story, I hope my journey will encourage you to pack your bags and start exploring!

Let’s see where the road takes us.

Originally from Cherkasy, a small Ukrainian town, I've always had a wanderlust spirit. Before embarking on my studies at LCC International University, I explored various cities across Ukraine.

Now, living in Lithuania, I've had the incredible opportunity to travel throughout Europe. With a growing network of international friends, there's always a new destination on my horizon.

While my travel experiences may not be as extensive as some, I've had the opportunity to explore some truly incredible destinations. From the vibrant cities of Europe to the breathtaking landscapes of the United States, each journey has been a unique adventure.

I'm passionate about sharing my stories, tips, and recommendations with others who have a love for travel. Whether you're planning your next trip or simply looking for inspiration, I hope my experiences will spark your wanderlust.
